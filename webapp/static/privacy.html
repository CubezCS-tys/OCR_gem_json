Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Who we are

ScanToText ("we", "our", "us") is an AI-powered document conversion service. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights regarding it.

2. Data we collect

  • Email address — obtained via Google Sign-In when you create an account. Used solely for authentication and customer support.
  • Stripe billing data — name, card details, and billing address collected and stored by Stripe. We receive only a customer ID and subscription status.
  • Uploaded documents — files you submit for OCR processing. These are stored temporarily (maximum 24 hours) on our servers solely for the purpose of generating your conversion output, then permanently deleted by our automated cleanup process.
  • Usage data — page counts processed per billing period. Stored in our database for quota enforcement.
  • Server logs — standard access logs (IP address, timestamp, HTTP status) retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging.

3. How we use your data

  • Provide and improve the OCR conversion service.
  • Manage subscriptions and enforce page quotas.
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g. fraud prevention, tax records).

We do not sell, share, or use your documents or email address for marketing or third-party advertising.

4. Third-party processors

Your documents are processed by the following AI services to generate conversion outputs:

  • Azure Document Intelligence (Microsoft) — for OCR and layout analysis.
  • Google Gemini — for semantic HTML output.
  • Mistral AI — for Markdown and image extraction.

Each provider processes document content only for the purpose of generating your requested output and under data processing agreements that prohibit training on your content. Please review their respective privacy policies for full details.

5. Data retention

  • Uploaded files and job results: deleted automatically within 24 hours of upload.
  • Account records (email, subscription status, page usage): retained while your account is active and for 90 days after deletion.
  • Stripe transaction records: retained as required by applicable tax law (typically 7 years).

6. Your rights (UK GDPR / GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data. To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use a single short-lived oauth_state cookie during Google sign-in to prevent CSRF attacks. No tracking or advertising cookies are set.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel. We use industry-standard practices to protect stored data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated by updating the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@scantotext.com
